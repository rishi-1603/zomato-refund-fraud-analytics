## Key findings

1. **Refund rates are stable across cohorts** (7.8%–10.4%). On this synthetic dataset, refund behavior is generated per-customer and doesn't depend on when they joined.
2. **Planted high-risk share is roughly uniform** across cohorts — the generator assigns risk independently of join date.
3. **On real data**, this analysis would reveal whether abuse emerges in specific acquisition periods (e.g., a fraud ring joining during a promo month), which is a critical fraud-detection signal.
4. **Method caveat:** synthetic, seeded dataset — the uniformity is by construction, not a finding about real customer behavior.
